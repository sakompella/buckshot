# Play patterns

Mean minimax regret of single-move choice patterns, from the deciding player's own information ([regret.md](regret.md)). Every figure here is generated by `tools/arena/analysis/patterns_regret.py` from the regret and knowledge databases of the 2,915-match snapshot of 2026-11-04: 2,895 scored matches with no DeepSeek seat. It is a casual pass, with means and no intervals. Treat a gap of a few points on 20 to 100 decisions as noise.

An earlier version of this page also listed 20 habits found by hand in the traces. They came from queries that were not kept, on a 2,118-match scope, so they cannot be regenerated or checked against this snapshot and were removed. [beliefs-and-items.md](beliefs-and-items.md) holds the generated tables for what each model knew when it shot and what became of its items.

## Patterns against regret

Each row is one choice pattern: the decisions where it could have happened are split into those it matched and the rest, with the mean regret of each. "Same configs" is the mean of the rest for the same model and effort mix as the matched ones, so a weak model's share does not explain a gap. "Known" means the deciding player's own log proves the shell. The certain-live and certain-blank rows pool all models; the clue source `combined` is left out. The last three rows are comparison moves that are common to every model, not one model's habit.

- **Costly.** Shooting yourself on a shell you can prove live averages 27.5 points when proven by the count, against 1.7 for the other shots there. The glass (21.5, only 19 shots) and phone (26.5) rows are as bad. Shooting the opponent on the last shell's certain blank is mildly worse than the rest, 3.6 against 2.0. Shooting the opponent on a certain blank before the last shell costs 19 against 0.8. Inverting a known live last shell to keep the turn costs 10 against 1.4, and a self-shot with the gun sawed 10 against 2.8. Throwing a beer at a certain live costs 6 against 3 for the rest.
- **Cost little.** Using the phone with two or fewer shells left averages 2.6. Sawing an unknown chamber averages 1.6, and sawing a certain live 0.4.
- **No clear gap.** Smoking your own cigarettes at full health averages 1.4 points outside Luna (1.2 while the opponent holds adrenaline), against 1.5 for the other moves there. Luna's 97 such smokes average 4.2 against 2.4, which is a small sample. Shooting yourself at exactly half live is 4.3 against 3.8 for shooting the opponent. Shooting yourself at an unknown chamber with under half live is 5.4 against 4.5 for the other shots there, so it shows no cost here.
- **Limits.** The item rows (smoking, phone, saws) are only as good as the evaluator's untrained item weights, so "cost little" there means the evaluator values the item little. Shots on the last shell end at the next reload, which the search scores as a static leaf. Many of the other rows have more than three shells left, where most searches stop early. Smoking counts only the player's own cigarettes, not ones stolen with adrenaline.
